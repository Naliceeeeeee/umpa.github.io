How to Get Started

  1. Reach out — Email or call UMPA with how you would like to serve.
  2. Meet our team — We discuss your gifts, time, and the programs that need support.
  3. Serve with training — Orientation and guidance so you can volunteer with confidence.
  4. Make an impact — Stand for life alongside ambassadors across Uganda.
