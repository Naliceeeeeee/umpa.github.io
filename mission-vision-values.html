Vision, Mission & Values

What guides our work to promote and defend human life in Uganda.

Our Vision & Mission

Vision

A population knowledgeable about the value of every human life and a society based on a genuine and authentic respect for every human life.

Mission

Commitment to promoting a culture of life.

Our Core Values

Human Dignity

Every Human person has inherent worth because they are created in the image and likeness of God.

Right to Life

The most fundamental human right from which all others flow.

Respect for Human Life

Honoring every human life as sacred and worthy of protection.

Integrity

Doing the right thing even when no one is watching.

Love and Compassion

Caring and serving others with kindness.
