Our Programs

Programs

Awareness campaigns, pro-life clubs, counseling, natural family planning, charity outreach, training, conventions, and media ministry — how UMPA serves youth, families, and parishes across Uganda.

Awareness

Awareness Campaigns

Interactive educative talks, videos, and debates that empower people of every age and background to embrace moral values in schools, churches, and communities.

Pro-Life Clubs

Pro-Life Clubs

Forming and mentoring pro-life youth clubs with prayer, peer education, drama, and outreach so young ambassadors stand for life on campus and in their communities.

Counseling

Counseling & Family Guidance

Psychosocial support and pro-life counselling for crisis pregnancy, post-abortion healing, families in crisis, addictions, and other life-related concerns.
