Who We Are

MISSION: COMMITMENT TO PROMOTING A CULTURE OF LIFE

We promote and defend human life from the moment of conception until natural death — through sensitization about anti-life practices and behaviors, offer counseling to those affected by said practices and upholding marriage and family as the foundation of society.

Our Vision & Mission

Vision

A population knowledgeable about the value of every human life and a society based on a genuine and authentic respect for every human life.

Mission

Committed to saving life and promoting a culture of life across Uganda.

What We Do

Awareness Campaigns & Pro-Life Clubs

Empowering people of all ages and backgrounds to embrace strong moral values through interactive talks, educational videos, meaningful discussions, and engaging question-and-answer sessions.

Learn More

Pro-Life Conventions & Diocesan Days

Young people from all pro-life clubs convene every March to celebrate life, grow in faith, and inspire others through creative expressions of pro-life values.

Learn More

Radio & TV Talk Shows

Since 2014, UMPA has hosted radio programs on Radio Maria Uganda and other Catholic media platforms such as Radio Sapientia, promoting the dignity of the human person and the family through reason, science, culture, and faith.

Learn More

Counseling

We offer guidance and counseling for women facing crisis pregnancies, post-abortion effects, individuals struggling with addictions, same-sex attraction, families in crisis and others facing personal family related difficulties.

Learn More

Natural Family Planning

We offer training in highly effective, modern and safe NFP methods that respect God’s design, help strengthen relationships, and can be used to achieve or postpone pregnancy.

Learn More

Charitable Activities

Each year UMPA ambassadors and most pro-life clubs organize and participate in charity activities aimed at extending kindness to the vulnerable.
